brainmaze-eeg
===================

The documentation of **brainmaze-eeg** (3.0.0) is maintained with the package
and is not duplicated here: https://bnelair.github.io/brainmaze-eeg/

It covers interictal spike detection (Janca and Barkmeier detectors and
``GapAwareSpikeDetector`` for recordings with NaN gaps), the ``WaveDetector``
slow-wave features, spectral and time-domain feature extraction, sleep
classifiers, hypnogram scoring, preprocessing and CRP analysis.

- **Install**: ``pip install brainmaze-eeg``
- **Repository**: https://github.com/bnelair/brainmaze-eeg
